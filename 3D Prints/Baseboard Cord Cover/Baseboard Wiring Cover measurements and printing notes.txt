Baseboard cord cover measurements and printing instructions

If printing in white resin, increase exposure time from 2sec to 4sec.

Measurements I made when building this file:
- Thickness of the AC power cord: 8.5mm
- 3d print wall thickness for resin printer: 2mm
- Channel Width Inner diameter: (to hold up to two AC cords): minimum 14.6mm
- Carpet to first ledge top 122mm high. Note: first ledge is the inflection crease, outer ledge edge is rounded 
- Tiny outer ledge edge curve from front wall to inflection crease: 1.2mm high, 1.2mm deep
- S-curve: First ledge Inflection crease to top shelf: 16mm high, 7mm deep
- Top shelf: 3.8mm deep
