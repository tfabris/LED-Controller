Master Bedroom custom lit shelves - measurements and sourcing
 
Desired width and depth of shelves:
- West wall: 16” deep 3’ (36") long, 2 stud brackets each
- North wall: 12” deep, 6’ (72") long, 3 stud brackets each

Shelf contents and other limitations:
- Largest book: 15” tall 10.5” deep
- TOS model: 16”w 16” tall 34” long, 20 pounds weight
- My electric box outlet, top edge, including plastic cover: 17"
- Approx 6’ height top shelf (top shelf approx 72”, can be a little higher).

Number of shelves = 4 shelves on each wall, height calculations
- 72” / 4 shelves = 18” gap between shelves, plus 2" thickness allowance for each shelf: 1" for shelf itself, 1" for lighting bracket, plus some slop.
- Bottom shelf top edge: 20"  (17" for outlet, 1" wood thickness, plus starting everyone 2” higher, so that everyone misses the stud nails I found)
- Second shelf top edge: 40" (20"+18"gap+2" thickness allowance) 
- Third shelf top edge:  60" (40"+18"gap+2" thickness allowance)
- Fourth shelf top edge: 80" (60"+18"gap+2" thickness allowance)

Studs
- 16 inch centers
- Scan with scanning tool and then locate individual stud nails with magnets.

Voltage
- MEAN WELL LRS-200-5 - Primary output voltage (vdc): 5 - Output current (a): 40 Maximum output power (w): 200 
- Make sure you understand the Live versus Neutral versus Ground pins for the main AC into the power supply https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/Enclosed_Type_EN.pdf
- Find out which fuses to use in your fuse holders:  https://quinled.info/2019/02/18/use-fuses-for-increased-safety/

Strip lengths
- Figure out how many LEDs per strip you'll be using based on your shelf lengths.
  - You'll need to know the exact numbers of LEDs per row, for the programming side of the project.
- Total strip is 300 LEDs in 5000mm
- That’s  60 LEDs per meter, or 1 led every 16.6666mm
- 1 foot is 304.8mm
- So about 18.3 LEDs per foot
- 16.4ft strips x2:
  - First strip: Get two longs and a short from it: 16.4ft - 6ft - 6ft - 3 = 1.4 feet left over.
  - Second strip: Get one long and the remaining two short strips out of it: 16.4ft - 6ft - 3ft - 3ft = 4.4ft left over.
- Final tally: at 60 LEDs per meter, minus about one LED per each end...
  - Shelf 1 = 67⅛in or 1.70688m = 102.4 (actual: 100 LEDs)
  - Shelf 2/3 = 71⅝in or 1.81864m = 109.1184 (actual: 106 and 107 LEDs, respectively)
  - Shelf 4/5/6 = 35⅞in or 0.90932m = 54.5592 (actual: 52 each)
  - 100 + 106 + 107 + 52 + 52 + 52 = 469 LEDs total
  - Array positions (0-indexed):
    - Shelf 1 = [0] to [99]
    - Shelf 2 = [100] to [205]
    - Shelf 3 = [206] to [312]
    - Shelf 4 = [313] to [364]
    - Shelf 5 = [365] to [416]
    - Shelf 6 = [417] to [468]
